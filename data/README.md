# ONS Reference Datasets

This directory contains official reference datasets published by the **Office for National Statistics (ONS)** used in the empirical analysis of the UK Private Non-Financial Corporation (PNFC) sector.

---

## Dataset Summaries

### 1. `2024q2referencetables.xlsx`
- **Title**: Profitability of UK Companies Reference Tables (2024 Q2)
- **Source**: [ONS Profitability Dataset](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/nationalaccounts/uksectoraccounts/datasets/profitabilityofukcompaniesreferencetable)
- **Key Sheets Used**:
  - `Table 2`: Net rate of return by sector (Manufacturing, Services, UK Continental Shelf, Non-Continental Shelf, and Total PNFCs).
- **Time Frequency**: Quarterly & Annual (1997 – 2024).

### 2. `nbsreferencetables2024.xlsx`
- **Title**: The National Balance Sheet Estimates (2024 Release)
- **Source**: [ONS National Balance Sheet Estimates](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/nationalaccounts/uksectoraccounts/datasets/thenationalbalancesheetestimates/current)
- **Key Sheets Used**:
  - `Table 3`: Non-Financial Corporations Balance Sheet (`S.11`).
  - `Table 4`: Public Non-Financial Corporations Balance Sheet (`S.11001`).
  - `Table 5`: Private Non-Financial Corporations Balance Sheet (`S.11002/3`).
- **Variables**: Non-financial assets (`Total Assets`), Financial liabilities (`Total Liabilities`), and `Net Worth`.
- **Time Frequency**: Annual (1995 – 2023).

### 3. `ukea2023q2table2corpssectorv2.xlsx`
- **Title**: UK Economic Accounts - Financial and Non-Financial Corporations Sector (2023 Q2)
- **Source**: [ONS UK Economic Accounts](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/nationalaccounts/uksectoraccounts/datasets/ukeconomicaccountsfinancialandnonfinancialcorporationssector/current)
- **Key Sheets Used**:
  - `3.1.7_B`: Capital Account, Non-Financial Corporations (`S.11`).
- **Variables**: `P.51g` Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF / Investment Amount).
- **Time Frequency**: Quarterly (1987 – 2023).
